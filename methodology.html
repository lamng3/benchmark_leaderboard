Ranking rules

Keep comparisons fair

  1. 01
    Same task

    Do not rank different evaluation objectives against one another.

  2. 02
    Same ontology pair

    Pair difficulty and overlap must remain visible.

  3. 03
    Same protocol

    Sampling, top-k, iteration count, and gold sets must match.

  4. 04
    Traceable setup

    Record the exact model version and workflow ordering.
